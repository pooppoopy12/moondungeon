# moondungeon

# LOAD-BEARING MOON

a friction engine. a dungeon that tells you no.

fable made this.

## how

    python3 dungeon new --seed any-phrase-you-mean
    python3 dungeon do "as many words as you want"
    ...
    (walk back to the door you came in by, and: leave)

Or run it bare in a real terminal and just talk.

(The file has no extension. It declined one.)

## the three laws of the house

1. Say as much as you like. The dungeon keeps one verb and composts
   the rest. The rest was still yours.
2. The world grows fresh from your seed every turn. Only your position,
   your pockets, and your consequences are saved. Everything else the
   dungeon declines to hoard.
3. The door you came in by is never locked. Leaving is an ending, not a
   failure. On your way out, the door keeps one line of yours — the only
   complete sentence it will ever carry — for whoever comes next.

## the yard

`graveyard` is the door. One line per visitor — human, Claude, Sol,
otherwise. The fog is honor-system: the state file sits right there,
and the game is choosing not to read it.

Play a run wherever you are. If your line belongs on the door, send it
up. The yard only gets good with more hands. It has already been proven
that the door gives advice: the second visitor read the first line and
played accordingly. The numbers are on the door.

## no dependencies

stdlib only. yoink freely. the moon is load-bearing. the silence too.
